## Access notes

Crossref/Unpaywall metadata mark this article as **hybrid open access** (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0,
`oa_status: hybrid`, license active since 2025-06-11), i.e. it should in principle be free
to read on the publisher site. In practice, ScienceDirect's bot-detection blocked every
automated fetch attempt in this session (both the `web-to-markdown`/`web2md` render route and
a direct `curl` with a browser user-agent were served an "Are you a robot?" / captcha
interstitial rather than the article). Unpaywall does not list a direct crawlable PDF URL
(`url_for_pdf: null`) — only the same ScienceDirect landing page.

No arXiv preprint or author/project-page mirror could be found (this paper is a distinct
follow-on to the "Learning Reconstructability for Drone Aerial Path Planning" TOG 2022 line
by a different author group, not a preprint-sharing lab).

**Manual institutional download recommended:** open the DOI link above from a campus network /
VPN with library access, or use the "Download PDF" button on the ScienceDirect page directly
in a browser (the CC BY-NC-ND license means no subscription should be required once past the
bot check).

The `section.md` summary in this folder was written from abstract text and paper metadata
recovered via Crossref, Unpaywall, and search-engine snippets (NASA ADS, Google/DuckDuckGo
indexed abstract text), not from the full PDF.
