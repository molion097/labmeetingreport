These papers trace how table structure recognition has evolved for messy, real-world images rather than clean scanned documents. The first paper introduces both the field's hardest benchmark and a detection-based baseline for it, and the ones that follow each attack a different weakness in that baseline, better boundary regression for distorted tables, bringing in the table's own text as a second signal, and pretraining the backbone on table-specific tasks before fine-tuning. Read in order they form a fairly clean line of incremental progress on the same benchmark.
