## CRAF: Cross-Modal Representation Alignment and Fusion for Speech Translation

Zhenbei Guo, Wenzhou Wu, Hua Lai, Yan Xiang, Yuxin Huang, Zhengtao Yu. Proceedings of the 24th China National Conference on Computational Linguistics (CCL 2025), pp. 1031-1042. https://aclanthology.org/2025.ccl-1.78/

End-to-end speech translation converts speech directly into another language's text without an intermediate transcription step, but speech representations run much longer than the matching text, so models have to compress them before feeding both modalities into a shared encoder, and prior methods tend to lose real information during that compression and then fuse the two modalities without any signal for which parts actually matter. CRAF targets both problems with two modules added on top of a pretrained HuBERT speech encoder. Its Dynamic Weighted Length-Adapter still compresses the speech sequence through two convolutional layers, but instead of only keeping the final layer's output, it cross-attends the second layer's features against the first layer's and learns a per-position weight that adaptively blends the shallow and cross-attended features rather than discarding the shallow layer outright. Its Representation Fusion Module first aligns the speech and text sequences with optimal transport rather than naive length matching, applies Mixup to blend the aligned representations, then runs self-attention over the mixed sequence so the model can weight the more informative positions instead of treating the fused sequence uniformly. Training follows a pretrain-then-finetune recipe, pretraining on text-only translation pairs, then jointly finetuning on speech-to-text and text-to-text translation together with a KL consistency term pulling the speech, text, and fused representations toward matching distributions. Across eight language pairs from the MuST-C benchmark, CRAF reaches an average BLEU of 29.0, about 0.3 points above the strongest comparison methods, with the largest single-pair gain reaching 0.5 BLEU. An ablation on the English-German pair shows each module contributes on its own, roughly 27.2 BLEU with only the length adapter and 27.3 with only the fusion module, with the full combination doing best. The overall improvement is modest rather than dramatic, which fits the paper's framing, it's fixing two specific failure modes in an already fairly mature pipeline rather than proposing a new paradigm.
