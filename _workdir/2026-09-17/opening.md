These sit around the same practical question: can current perception systems actually be trusted to find lane and road boundaries once the picture gets messy. The VLM survey is the skeptic here, showing vision-language models often answer driving questions without really looking at the image. LaDeL takes the opposite bet, building lane detection directly on a language model's reasoning. GlareLane exposes a specific real-world blind spot, glare and abrupt lighting, that clean benchmarks miss. LINEA sits underneath all of it, a general line detector showing smarter attention design closes more of the speed-accuracy gap than raw scale does.
