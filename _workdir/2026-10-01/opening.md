This set is about getting machines to understand everyday sound, from the low-level job to the language-level one. The location-aware HAR paper is the practical edge case, adapting a small sound model to each room on the device. GAMA and Audio Flamingo 2 are the general-purpose audio-language backbones, and the second largely answers the first with better data and cross-attention. EchoScriptor and the soundscape captioning paper then use language models to turn audio into something a person can read, a day's narrative in one case and how a place feels in the other.
