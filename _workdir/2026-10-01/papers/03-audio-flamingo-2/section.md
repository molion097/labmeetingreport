## Audio Flamingo 2: An Audio-Language Model with Long-Audio Understanding and Expert Reasoning Abilities

Ghosh et al. (NVIDIA and University of Maryland), ICML 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03983

Audio-language models do fine on classification and captioning but stay weak on expert-level reasoning about sounds and music, and almost none of them handle audio beyond about 30 seconds. Audio Flamingo 2 argues the fix is better data and a better audio representation rather than a bigger model. It pairs a 203M audio encoder (AF-CLAP, a CLAP model retrained on over 8M audio-caption pairs with an extra contrastive term for caption paraphrases) with a 3B decoder LM, joined by gated cross-attention. The authors build AudioSkills, a synthetic QA set targeting seven reasoning skills, and train in three stages, ending with a long-audio stage on their LongAudio set of 260k QA pairs for clips up to 5 minutes. They also release LongAudioBench, with 2,429 expert-reviewed items. AF2 beats Gemini Flash v2 on MMAU sound (65.1 vs 61.7) and music (72.9 vs 56.5), and on LongAudioBench scores 64.2 against 45.3. Retrained on the same data, the 7B GAMA reaches only 52.8 MMAU average versus 69.0 for AF2, which suggests cross-attention and the audio encoder matter. The gains lean on synthetic LLM-written QA, so the evaluation is only as reliable as that pipeline.
