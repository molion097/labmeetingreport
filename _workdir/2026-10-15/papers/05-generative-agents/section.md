## Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior

Joon Sung Park, Joseph C. O'Brien, Carrie J. Cai, Meredith Ringel Morris, Percy Liang, Michael S. Bernstein. UIST 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442

Earlier LLM agents could produce a plausible line of dialogue but not a coherent life over time. This paper wraps ChatGPT in an architecture with a memory stream, a natural-language log of experiences retrieved by recency, importance, and relevance, plus reflection that condenses memories into higher-level inferences, and planning that breaks a daily agenda into finer actions and revises it when events occur. Twenty-five agents populate Smallville, a Sims-like sandbox. In a study, 100 Prolific raters ranked interview answers from the full system, three ablations, and a crowdworker baseline. The full system scored highest (TrueSkill 29.89), ahead of no reflection (26.88), no reflection or planning (25.64), crowdworkers (22.95), and a memoryless baseline (21.21), an effect size of about eight standard deviations, and only crowdworkers versus that baseline were not significantly different. Over two simulated days, knowledge of a party spread from one agent to thirteen, network density rose from 0.167 to 0.74, and five of twelve invitees attended. Agents sometimes missed memories or embellished them. It is the base architecture that the education simulation papers here build on.
